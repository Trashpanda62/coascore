Follistatin-344 COA: molecular weight and what to check

Follistatin-344 is listed in CoaScore's reference data as a myostatin-inhibitor glycoprotein; other names include FST-344, FS-344. This page collects the reference numbers a certificate of analysis for it should be compared against, and the cautions that go with them.

Why there is no single molecular weight

CoaScore's reference data deliberately lists no single mass for this one. 344-residue glycoprotein isoform of follistatin (~37.8 kDa core, higher glycosylated). A full protein, not a defined-MW peptide — identity by SDS-PAGE/immunoassay. Also sold as FST-315 (different isoform). A COA that states one exact molecular weight for it deserves a question about where the number came from.

Cautions specific to this compound

  • Salt and counter-ion. Recombinant glycoprotein; not a small-molecule salt form.

What a COA for Follistatin-344 should show

  1. Identity. The report names the method it used for identity and says what it was compared against.
  2. Purity. The report shows the HPLC trace, the method conditions and a peak table, not only a percentage. CoaScore's reference data notes: ≥ 95% (SDS-PAGE).
  3. Quantity. If the vial states an amount, the report says how it was measured. Purity and quantity are different numbers.
  4. Traceability. Lot number, sample ID, issuing lab, dates and report number match the vial and appear on every page of the PDF.
