Terms, privacy & disclaimer

CoaScore reads documentation for research materials. It does not turn a PDF into a laboratory result, and it does not make decisions for you.

CoaScore does not provide human-use instructions or dose recommendations. Do not use a documentation score as evidence that a material is safe, suitable for use in humans, authentic, uncontaminated, or appropriate for a particular purpose.

Terms of use

You are responsible for checking source documents, laboratory records, calculations, and decisions made from the output. Automated extraction can misread text, units, tables, or images. Scores describe the documentation supplied to the tool under its stated criteria; they are not laboratory results, certifications, warranties, purchasing recommendations, or substitutes for qualified analytical review.

Use the site lawfully and do not rely on it as the sole basis for a consequential decision. The site and its content are provided as available without a promise that every output will be complete or error-free.

Your files stay local

The COA Interpreter and calculators run client-side in your browser. Documents and values opened in those tools are processed on your device and are not uploaded by the tools. This statement does not cover third-party links, browser extensions, network monitoring, or a future feature that discloses a different data flow before use.
